Role ecosystem

Each person sees the right workspace for the job.

Customer

Shops products, tracks orders, rates deliveries, and manages payment choices.

Staff

Picks items, updates readiness, and can approve release if managers are unavailable.

Driver

Handles delivery handoff, route progress, and cash code confirmation at delivery.

Manager

Oversees catalogue, fulfilment review, delivery release, and store-level performance.

Admin

Controls stores, categories, products, roles, and system-wide oversight metrics.

Payments and trust

Flexible checkout with safer delivery confirmation.

Online payment via Paystack

SmartGrocery uses Paystack’s secure hosted checkout for card and mobile money flows, keeping sensitive payment entry outside the app’s local form layer.

  • Backend-initialised transaction references
  • Server-side verification before payment success is accepted
  • Saved payment methods available for future checkout upgrades

Cash on delivery with verification

Cash payments are not marked complete until the delivery driver confirms the customer’s one-time cash code. This reduces false completion and wrong handoff risk.
